3. Position Strategy Snapshot

  • Early Position (UTG): Play tight. Only premium pairs (AA-TT) and big broadways (AKs, AQs).
  • Middle Position: Open up to suited connectors (98s+) and medium pairs (99-77).
  • Late Position (Button/Cutoff): Widest opening range. Steal blinds with suited aces and broadways.
